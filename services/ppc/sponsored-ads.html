Sponsored Ads Management

Sponsored Products carries the revenue load, Sponsored Brands defends the brand and proves video, and Sponsored Display closes the loop, because the three ad types only perform when they run as one system where every dollar of spend maps to a decision I can defend.

How the Three Ad Types Divide the Work

Sponsored Products carries the revenue load in every account I run, because it is the only ad type that meets a shopper at the exact moment of a search with purchase intent behind it. Sponsored Brands earns its budget by defending the brand name and by testing video creative in an auction that gives fast and honest feedback, which matters later because no video reaches my DSP flights until it has already won here. Sponsored Display closes the loop by defending my own product pages and by retargeting shoppers who viewed a listing and left, so the three types operate as one system where each covers a weakness of the others rather than as three separate line items competing for the same budget.

The SKAG Philosophy

Every term that matters gets its own single keyword campaign in exact match, because when one keyword owns one campaign, attribution becomes unambiguous and every dollar of spend maps to a decision I can defend. That isolation is what turns bid management into deterministic rules instead of judgment calls, since a campaign that contains one keyword produces numbers that mean exactly one thing. The structure also gives each term its own budget ceiling, which stops a single expensive keyword from quietly draining the budget that ten profitable terms were supposed to share.

The Bid Rules and Their Cadence

Every week I sort every keyword into top performers, bloated targets, and bleeders against your target ACoS, and that one sort drives everything that follows. Bleeders get cut on tiered click thresholds, winners get pushed, and every new bid anchors to the CPC the auction actually charged rather than to the bid someone once guessed, because the auction price is evidence and the old bid is only an opinion. The search term report gets harvested on the same cadence, so winning terms graduate into their own exact match campaigns and get negated back at the source in the same motion, and every change lands in a log with the date, the old value, the new value, and the reason.

What This Produces

This is the structure that carried Vermont Smoke & Cure past $331K in a single month and later ran the account profitably at a 6.60% TACoS, and it is the same discipline that walked DigDefence Solutions down from a 30.79% TACoS baseline into the low teens while the business grew. The outcomes follow from the structure, because a system where every bid has a reason compounds week after week in a way that heroic manual effort never does.
