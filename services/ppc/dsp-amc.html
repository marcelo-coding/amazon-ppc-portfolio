Amazon DSP and AMC

I run DSP as an investment rather than a branding expense, because every flight opens with remarketing, every creative proves itself in Sponsored Brands first, and every audience gets matched to the product that makes sense for it.

Remarketing Funds Everything Else

Every DSP flight I run opens with zero upper funnel spend, because shoppers who viewed a product page or abandoned a cart have already declared intent, and display advertising is excellent at finishing a sale that was already in motion. Budgets follow a 60/30/10 split where the largest share belongs to that remarketing core, and the smaller shares expand outward only into audiences the core has already proven can convert, so the flight earns its way up the funnel instead of buying awareness on faith. Video holds to the same standard, since a creative that cannot win in Sponsored Brands has not earned programmatic inventory at programmatic prices.

AMC Audiences Standard Targeting Cannot Reach

Amazon Marketing Cloud is where the engagement gets its edge, because AMC lets me build audiences from query level signals that the standard DSP console cannot see. I assemble segments like shoppers who viewed a product repeatedly without purchasing, buyers of one product in the catalog who have never tried its natural companion, and past purchasers approaching the reorder window for a consumable, and each audience gets matched to the specific product that makes sense for it rather than to a funnel stage. That matching rule matters more than any bid setting, because an audience only converts when the product in front of it answers something true about who those shoppers actually are.

Measurement That Survives Scrutiny

DSP reporting fails most brands because it celebrates reach while the profit and loss statement quietly disagrees, so my measurement separates promoted sales from halo sales, tracks new to brand reach as a real number rather than a slogan, and reports everything against your profitability targets. When a line item stops returning revenue, its budget moves to one that does, and the change gets logged with its reason the same way a bid change does in sponsored ads. DSP enters the engagement at the day 30 reinvestment conversation, because upper funnel work is funded by the TACoS points the cleanup phase earns rather than by hope.

What This Produces

The outcome is a DSP program that behaves like an investment instead of a branding expense, where every audience has a reason to exist, every creative has already proven itself in a cheaper auction, and the monthly report reconciles against revenue you can find in your own statements. Brands come to me for a second opinion on DSP proposals precisely because this doctrine catches the awareness heavy plans that would have burned a quarter of budget before anyone asked what it returned.
