Amazon PPC

Connect Amazon DSP to AMC

I run DSP as an investment, not a branding expense. Flights open with remarketing. Creatives earn their slot by proving themselves in Sponsored Brands first, and I match each audience to the product that actually fits it.

Remarketing Pays for the Rest

Every DSP flight opens with zero upper funnel spend. Shoppers who viewed a product page or abandoned a cart already showed intent, and display is built to finish a sale that is already moving. Budgets follow a 60/30/10 split with the largest share in that remarketing core. The smaller shares expand only into audiences the core has already proven can convert, so the flight earns its way up the funnel instead of buying awareness on faith. Video follows the same rule. A creative that cannot win in Sponsored Brands has not earned programmatic inventory at programmatic prices.

AMC Audiences that Standard Targeting Cannot Reach

AMC gives me query level signals the standard DSP console cannot see, so I can build audiences the console misses. I segment shoppers who viewed a product repeatedly without purchasing, buyers of one product in the catalog who have never tried its natural companion, and past purchasers approaching the reorder window for a consumable. Each audience gets matched to the product that fits it, not to a funnel stage. That match beats any bid setting, because an audience converts only when the product in front of it answers something true about who those shoppers are.

Measurement That Survives Scrutiny

Most DSP reporting cheers on reach while the profit and loss statement says otherwise. I separate promoted sales from halo sales, track new to brand reach as a real number, and report against your profitability targets. When a line item stops returning revenue, I pull its budget to one that does and log the move with the reason, the same way a bid change gets logged in sponsored ads. DSP shows up at the day 30 reinvestment conversation, because upper funnel work gets funded by the TACoS points the cleanup phase earns, not by hope.

What You Get

You get a DSP program that runs like an investment, not a branding expense. Every audience exists for a reason. Each creative has already proven itself in a cheaper auction. The monthly report reconciles to revenue you can find in your own statements. Brands ask me for a second opinion on DSP proposals because this doctrine flags the awareness heavy plans that would have burned a quarter of budget before anyone asked what they returned.
