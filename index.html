My Process

How I take over an account and grow it

This is the operating procedure I run on every engagement, because a repeatable process is the only thing that produces repeatable results.

1

Budget and Guardrails

I set the starting budget at your last 30 days of spend plus 10 to 20 percent and begin spending on day one, because targets should come from your historicals while you only supply guardrails like margin and spend comfort.

2

Score and Forecast

I score the account on ad spend, CPC, conversion rate, and organic sales share, then build a 90 day revenue forecast whose key assumptions act as the contract between the plan and reality.

3

21 Day Cleanup

In the first 21 days I cut wasted spend by pausing bleeders, bloated targets, and zero impression campaigns, and I reallocate every freed dollar as I learn where your spend actually concentrates.

4

Day 30 Reinvestment

At day 30 I present the efficiency win, confirm the true budget and the working TACoS guardrail, and reinvest every saved point into upper funnel tests and scaling instead of banking it.

5

Product Level Profitability

From there I manage in margin dollars per unit, so I flag losing products and neglected winners, harvest converting search terms, and bid against a profitable CPC ceiling built from price, fees, and margin.

6

Monthly Reviews and Events

Every month I enter actuals by the third business day, investigate any variance beyond ten percent, and re-score the account, while dedicated event playbooks take over during windows like Prime Day.
