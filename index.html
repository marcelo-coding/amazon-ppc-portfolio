About

The person behind the dashboards

I am Marc, and I have spent the last ten years inside Amazon e-commerce, first on the seller side and then as a full time advertising specialist. Along the way I helped take a product launch to $3M in revenue within its first ten months, and that experience taught me that growth only counts when the margin survives it.

Today I manage advertising for 15 to 20 accounts at any given time, spanning Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, Amazon DSP, and AMC. Across that portfolio I hold an average ACoS of 25% on six figure PPC sales, and I can carry that load because I refuse to do repetitive work by hand. The bid rules, the report pipelines, and the campaign hygiene in my accounts all run as code that I wrote, versioned, and published.

If you want an advertising partner who treats your budget like an engineering problem with a paper trail, we will probably get along. The fastest way to find out is a short call where we look at your account together and I tell you exactly what I would change first.
