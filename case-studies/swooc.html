Case Study

SWOOC

Outdoor Games · Engagement data from November to December 2024

Outdoor Games

PPC Sales Up 76.86% in One Month While ACoS Dropped 23%

+$233,949.66 in PPC sales from November to December 2024, on only 36.12% more spend, with ACoS falling 8.16 points and TACoS falling 3.72 points.

December is the month when most Amazon accounts buy their growth, because holiday traffic invites teams to open the budgets and accept whatever efficiency they get. The challenge on SWOOC was to capture the December surge without letting spend scale as fast as sales, which meant the account needed structural controls in place before the peak rather than reactive budget pushes during it.

In early December I implemented campaign budget management driven by an ACoS filter, so budget flowed toward campaigns that earned it, and I paused campaign groups where inventory was running low so spend never piled into products that could not convert at full velocity. In the days before Christmas I added negative phrase targets across four Sponsored Brands campaign groups to cut wasted clicks at the peak, and I ran a product level ACoS threshold analysis comparing December 2023 to December 2024 so the budget rules were set against real historical performance. I closed the loop with a TACoS by SKU report covering November and December of both years, which kept every budget decision tied to item level truth rather than account averages.

From November to December 2024 PPC sales grew by $233,949.66, an increase of 76.86%, while ad spend grew by only $38,964.48, an increase of 36.12%. Because sales scaled more than twice as fast as spend, ACoS fell 8.16 percentage points, a 23.02% reduction, and TACoS fell 3.72 points, an 18.68% reduction. Total sales across the account rose $364,654.50, up 67.37%, which shows the advertising gains carried the whole business with them rather than cannibalizing organic volume.
