Alpha Tech Pet

Pet Care · Engagement data from January 2025 through July 2026

Pet Care

A 4.36 DSP ROAS, Then $275K to $372K in Monthly Revenue While TACoS Fell from 14.2% to 9.4%

February 2025 DSP delivered $44,973 in attributed revenue on $10,318 of spend for a 4.36 total ROAS, and a year later June 2026 closed at $372,419 in revenue, up 16.9% year over year, on a 9.4% TACoS.

Alpha Tech Pet, a pet care disinfectant brand, had a profitability problem hiding inside a growth story. Sales were moving in the right direction, but TACoS was sitting around 15 to 16%, which was heavier than the client's contribution margin targets could support, and a meaningful share of the sponsored products budget was going to spend that produced nothing. The challenge was to bring the ad cost down without starving the sales momentum that the account had worked hard to build.

I treated the ad mix as one system rather than two separate channels. I ran the DSP program with active budget rebalancing against the client's profitability targets, moving money toward the audiences and placements that were actually returning revenue, and I planned the split between sponsored products and DSP deliberately so that both contribution margin and sales growth were protected instead of trading one for the other. At the same time I gradually cut the wasted sponsored products spend, trimming it in stages so the account never lost auction presence on the terms that mattered.

The DSP side proved itself first, with January 2025 posting a 3.05 total ROAS and February climbing to 4.36 on $44,973 in DSP attributed revenue against $10,318 of spend. March 2025 then closed at $279,644.25 in ordered product sales, up 10.4% over February and 3.3% over the prior year, while the wasted spend cuts brought TACoS from roughly 15 to 16% down to 12.3% month to date by late March.

The 2026 Arc: Growth and Efficiency Compounding

The strongest storyline in this account is what happened over the following year, because revenue and efficiency improved at the same time for six straight measured months. January 2026 opened at $275,000 in revenue, up 11.2% year over year, on a 14.2% TACoS, and by June 2026 the account was doing $372,419, up 16.9% year over year, on a 9.4% TACoS. The TACoS walked down through 13.35% in March and 12.27% in May on its way to single digits, which means the account added roughly $97K of monthly revenue while the advertising cost per dollar of sales fell by a third.

"DSP campaign (launched Feb 14): 55K impressions, 19% click-through rate, 42K new-to-brand detail page views at $0.20 cost per view... DSP TROAS of 11 outperforming sponsored ads' TROAS of 7."

What It Proved

The account demonstrated the outcome every brand actually wants, which is efficiency and growth compounding together rather than competing. By mid July 2026 the account was pacing at 99.9% of its monthly goal on a 9.94% TACoS, which means the profitability problem that started the engagement is now a structural advantage.
