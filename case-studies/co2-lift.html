Case Study

CO2 Lift

Beauty & Personal Care · Engagement data from September through November 2025

Beauty & Personal Care

From $71K to the First $113K Month in Two Months, at 10.68% TACoS Against a 15% Target

Monthly sales grew 59% from September to November 2025, with November up 41.05% month over month and 62.01% year over year, while TACoS held at 10.68% against a 15.00% ceiling.

The Products Behind the Numbers

CO2 Lift sells professional grade carboxy therapy skincare, and the three listings below are the ones the growth ran through, because a single treatment mask and a daily moisturizer need very different keyword strategies.

Product images are the live Amazon catalog images for the listings described above.

Heading into Q4 2025 the account was coming off a soft late summer, with September sales at $71,636 after two straight down months, and the mandate was to build real holiday momentum without breaking the 15% TACoS ceiling the brand operates under. That combination rules out the usual playbook of buying Q4 growth with loose budgets, so the ramp had to come from creative and timing rather than raw spend.

The October data showed Sponsored Brand Video ads outperforming Sponsored Products, so I committed to a video led strategy and launched Beauty Event campaigns early to start compounding momentum ahead of Black Friday and Cyber Monday. In November I launched new Sponsored Brand Video campaigns on keywords that had historically converted best in Q4, and I deliberately kept spend conservative before the event window so the budget landed where the traffic was richest. I secured a special Turkey 12 placement for the brand after validating that inventory could support it, and I aligned Brand Tailored Promotions with Sponsored Brands and the Storefront so shoppers met one unified promotional experience, which lifted conversion on the discounted items.

November 2025 closed at $113,929, the first month in the account's history over $100K, up 41.05% from October and 62.01% over the prior November, and the growth cost nothing in efficiency because TACoS came in at 10.68% against the 15.00% target. The Turkey 12 event alone drove $70,989.42 in sales, up 139.12% from the prior year, and the coupons I ran ahead of the event added another $7,973.85 on top of that.
