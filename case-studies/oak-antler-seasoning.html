Grocery & Gourmet · Account under active management

Grocery & Gourmet

Oak & Antler Seasoning Company account under management

Full service Amazon advertising management for an early stage seasoning brand. Daily sales and TACoS are tracked against a defined blended efficiency target.

Products behind the numbers

Oak & Antler Seasoning Company sells small batch seasoning blends and bundles. Daily sales and TACoS tracking anchor to the three listings below, because bundles do not behave like single jars on conversion or margin.

These are the live Amazon catalog images for the listings above.

Oak & Antler Seasoning Company is early stage, so my job is disciplined foundation work, not headline growth. I run the Amazon advertising account end to end. Daily I track sales, spend, and TACoS, steer blended TACoS to the target the client and I set, and report on a regular cadence so decisions come from current numbers, not guesses.

On a young account I guard performance as much as I push it. I test campaign structure changes in small steps, measure the effect on real results, and reverse any change that does not earn its place. I did that here when a restructure underperformed. Measurement first keeps ad spend accountable while the catalog and the sales base build.
