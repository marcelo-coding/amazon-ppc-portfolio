Meet Marc

Amazon PPC expert and software developer helping sellers scale profitably through data-driven advertising strategies and custom automation tools.

My Story

I started my career at the intersection of software development and digital marketing. After years of building tools for e-commerce businesses, I recognized a massive gap in how Amazon sellers managed their PPC campaigns — most were either overspending or leaving money on the table.

That realization led me to build AI-powered optimization tools and offer hands-on PPC management services specifically for Amazon sellers. Today, I combine deep technical expertise with proven advertising strategies to deliver results that speak for themselves.

My approach is simple: use data and machine learning to eliminate wasted ad spend, identify high-converting keywords, and scale campaigns profitably. Every decision is backed by numbers, not guesswork.
