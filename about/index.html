About

Marcelo Siblag III, known as Marc Siblag

TACoS on my flagship accounts stays under 15 percent because the daily bid work in my accounts runs as code, not guesswork. I am Marc Siblag, an Amazon Ads Director and automation engineer with ten years in Amazon e-commerce. Across Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, Amazon DSP, and AMC, I run 15 to 20 accounts at a time.

The name on the paperwork is Marcelo Siblag III, the name on the campaigns is Marc Siblag, and people who have worked with me have written it as Marcelo Siblag, Marc Siblag III and Marcelo Siblag IV over the years, so every one of those spellings points at the same operator running the same Amazon accounts.

Every account, tool, hour, and routine is a system I measure and improve. My best work hits problems that span a whole account or a whole company, not isolated tactics. Teams come back for the mental models, because a model tells you what to do next quarter while a trick only tells you what to do today. The daily work keeps graduating into software for that reason. Any decision I have made the same way three times is one I would rather encode once and audit forever.
